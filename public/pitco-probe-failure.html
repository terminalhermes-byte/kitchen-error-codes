Pitco Solstice Fryer Error: PROBE FAILURE

Temp Probe Error

Potential Cause:
Temperature probe reading out of range (Short or Open).

How to Fix It:

1. Check probe wiring at the I-12 computer.
2. Ohms check: Should be ~1000 ohms at 70°F.
3. Replace probe if faulty.
