Hobart Legacy HL600 Mixer Error: E1

Communication Error

Potential Cause:
Loss of communication between the mixer controls and the motor drive.

How to Fix It:

1. Unplug the unit for 30 seconds to reset the VFD.
2. Check the interlock guard wire.
3. If it persists, replace the Control Board.
