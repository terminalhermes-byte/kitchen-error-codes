Hobart Legacy HL600 Mixer Error: OL1

Motor Overload

Potential Cause:
The motor is drawing too much current, likely due to a heavy batch or stiff dough.

How to Fix It:

1. Turn the mixer OFF.
2. Reduce the batch size.
3. Wait 2 minutes for the motor to cool.
4. Restart on a lower speed.
