TurboChef Sota / i3 / i5 Error: F1

Blower Motor Failure

Potential Cause:
The main convection blower is not spinning or the sensor is bad.

How to Fix It:

1. Check for obstructions in the fan.
2. Spin the fan manually (unplugged!) to check bearings.
3. Replace Blower Motor Controller.
