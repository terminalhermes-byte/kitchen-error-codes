Bunn Ultra-2 Slush Machine Error: Torque Error

Auger Torque Error

Potential Cause:
The slush is too thick/frozen, overloading the motor.

How to Fix It:

1. Turn machine OFF for 30 mins to thaw.
2. Adjust the thickness setting screw (counter-clockwise).
3. Check syrup-to-water ratio (too much water freezes hard).
