Taylor C602 Soft Serve Error: HPCO

High Pressure Cut Out

Potential Cause:
Compressor discharge pressure too high (overheating).

How to Fix It:

1. Clean the air filter screens.
2. Ensure proper airflow clearance.
3. Reset the High Pressure switch manually.
