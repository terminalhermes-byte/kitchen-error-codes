Alto-Shaam Cook & Hold Oven Error: E-70

Probe Error (Short)

Potential Cause:
Temperature probe circuit is shorted.

How to Fix It:

1. Check for pinched wires in the door hinge.
2. Replace probe if resistance is near 0 ohms.
