Blodgett Zephaire Convection Oven Error: FAN DELAY

Blower Fan Delay

Potential Cause:
Blower not reaching speed or door switch open.

How to Fix It:

1. Check door switch alignment.
2. Inspect blower wheel for rubbing/obstruction.
3. Check blower motor windings.
