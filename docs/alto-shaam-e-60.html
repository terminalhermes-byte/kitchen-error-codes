Alto-Shaam Cook & Hold Oven Error: E-60

Probe Error (Open)

Potential Cause:
Temperature probe circuit is open.

How to Fix It:

1. Check probe connection at the control block.
2. Inspect probe tip for damage.
3. Replace probe if infinite resistance.
