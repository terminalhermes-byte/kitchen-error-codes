Scotsman Prodigy Ice Machine Error: 2

Maximum Harvest Time

Potential Cause:
Ice formed but did not drop within 3.5 minutes.

How to Fix It:

1. Check harvest assist solenoid operation.
2. Inspect evaporator plate for scale/calcium.
3. Verify curtain switch sensitivity.
