Rational SelfCookingCenter (SCC) Error: 40

Care Pump Error

Potential Cause:
The Care (descaling) pump is not running or is blocked.

How to Fix It:

1. Check the Care pump for blockages (scale chunks).
2. Verify the level electrode is clean.
3. Replace the Care pump.
