Vulcan PowerFry VK Series Error: HI LIMIT

High Limit Tripped

Potential Cause:
Oil temperature exceeded 450°F (232°C). Safety shutdown.

How to Fix It:

1. Allow oil to cool below 400°F.
2. Press the red reset button inside the cabinet (behind the door).
3. If it trips again immediately, replace the High Limit thermostat.
