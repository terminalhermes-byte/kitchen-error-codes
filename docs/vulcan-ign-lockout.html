Vulcan PowerFry VK Series Error: IGN LOCKOUT

Ignition Lockout

Potential Cause:
The fryer failed to light after 3 attempts.

How to Fix It:

1. Turn the power switch OFF.
2. Check if the gas valve is ON.
3. Wait 30 seconds.
4. Turn power ON to reset ignition module.
